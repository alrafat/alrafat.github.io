## The problem

In Bangladesh, finding out *when* and *where* a specialist sits for consultation usually means calling several hospitals or relying on word of mouth. Chamber schedules are scattered across many hospital websites.

## What it does

**Doctor Kothay** (“where is the doctor?”) brings chamber times from official hospital websites into one search. Type something like *“heart doctor tomorrow”* or *“skin doctor Dhanmondi”* and it shows every matching specialist who is sitting on that day — with the time, the hospital and how to call for a serial.

- Search by specialty, hospital, area or time
- Government hospitals and diagnostic tests
- Bilingual: English and Bangla (কখন কোন ডাক্তার কোথায় বসছেন?)
